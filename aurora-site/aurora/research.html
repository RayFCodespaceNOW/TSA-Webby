Research questions

What we still want to know

Mars still has plenty of unanswered questions. Here are a few worth investigating.

Want More Information?

  1. What equipment is needed for Mars exploration?

  2. What tools would be necessary for surface operations, and what measurements would they provide?

  3. What life-forms have been discovered on Mars?

  4. What effect do these life forms have on the actual planet and atmosphere around it?

  5. How should Martian sites be managed?

  6. What practices and procedures could countries do to create safer experiences?

Data Should Be Available For Review

Clear reporting makes it easy to access data about mission strategy, cost, limitations, and results. Public findings and international cooperation are also careful considerations.

AURORA is a fictional educational concept. It is not an agency, active mission, or launch schedule.

Research Beyond Earth Has Its Limits

Work on science, life support, medicine, and materials may inform research on Earth. All though this does not translate into direct benefits.

Space Exploration involves dangerous risks and heavy costs. Future goals on exploration should be decided on science goals, public opinion, crew safety, and cost.
