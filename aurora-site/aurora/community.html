Things a class could try

  1. Draw a spacecraft.
    Ask students to include a place to sleep, a way to make power and one special feature they invented.
  2. Design a Mars rover.
    Choose wheels, tools and a camera, then explain what each part would help explore.
  3. Plan a safe landing site.
    Mark rocks, slopes and areas worth studying on a shared paper map.
  4. Send a message to the crew.
    Write one question or encouraging note for a fictional Mars crew.
  5. Build a classroom Mars base.
    Use recycled boxes and paper to make a model, then label what each room is for.

How a school project could work

Start small

Ask a teacher whether one class would like to try a short drawing or design activity. Share a simple prompt, give students time to create, and invite them to write a sentence about their idea.

Make a classroom gallery

Display the work at school first. If the school and families approve sharing it, choose a few pieces for a community post and explain what the students designed.

Keep students safe: Get school and parent or guardian permission before sharing student work. Leave out children's full names, faces, schedules and identifying details. These sample posts are fictional; no school event or partnership is being claimed.

AURORA is a fictional project about Mars exploration, not a real mission or spacecraft program.
