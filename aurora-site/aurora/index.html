Five things to know about the destination

Figures from the European Space Agency’s Mars fact page. See the Reference page.

One Mars day
24 h 37 minCalled a “sol.” Close to an Earth day.
Surface gravity
3.7 m/s²About 38% of what you feel on Earth.
Atmosphere
95% CO₂Mostly carbon dioxide, so humans cannot breathe it.
Width
6,794 kmRoughly half the width of Earth.
One Mars year
687 daysA little under two Earth years.
