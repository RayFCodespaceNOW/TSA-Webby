Fictional mission · Educational concept

Four people. One planet. Hundreds of days from home.

AURORA is a made-up expedition that asks a real question: what would it take to send people to Mars, and bring them back? Follow the mission from launch to return.

Follow the mission Explore the spacecraft

Why it matters

Mars is the next hard question for human exploration.

Robots have studied Mars for decades. People have never been there. A crewed mission would be the longest, farthest trip humans have ever attempted, with no quick way home if something goes wrong.

That makes Mars a good teacher. Every problem on the trip, such as air, water, radiation, health and teamwork, is a problem engineers and scientists on Earth are working on right now.

This site is our team’s way of showing how those problems connect, and where the honest answer is still “we don’t know yet.”
