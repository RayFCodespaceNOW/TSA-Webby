Crew roster

The four who would go

Real expeditions succeed because of the people as much as the machines. These crew members are fictional, and each one stands for a skill a Mars mission would need.

Meet the crew

Person 1:

Njeri Mwangi. Black Female Commander. Born in Nairobi, Kenya and immigrated to the United States to teach chemistry at Princeton University. Soon went to work on the Artemis Vl mission, then helped test multiple Mars environments and probes, to later get promoted to the Aurora space exploration program.

Person 2:

Hiroto Yamazaki. Japanese Male Doctor and Scientist. Born in Hiroshima Japan, he studied sports medicine at the University of Tokyo, where he also learnt English. Eventually the Aurora space exploration program recruited him.

Person 3:

Gonzales Lacasa. Mexican Pilot. He was Born in Mexico City, Mexico. When he turned 14, his parents immigrated to the United States for better job opportunities. He got very interested in his fathers passion about the stars and space, so he studied for years to later apply for the Massachusetts Institute of Technology and got accepted to later become a pilot for Aurora.

Person 4:

Logan Smith. Flight Engineer. Logan was born in Burlington, Vermont. He had great interest in mechanics, and generally how things work, from a very young age. When he entered the portal for a flight mechanics camp through his middle school, he discovered his strengths and was off to the races.

Several Areas of Expertise is Required.

A Mars team would need science, medicine, piloting, and engineering experience and practice to be a successful team under high pressure situations.

These profiles are fictional and do not represent any astronauts or selected people
